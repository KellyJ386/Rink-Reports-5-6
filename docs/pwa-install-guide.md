# Install Rink Reports on Your Phone

Rink Reports works like a regular app once you add it to your home screen.
You only need to do this once. After that, tap the Rink Reports icon like
any other app — no browser, no typing the address, and you stay logged in.

Install it on the phone you carry during your shift. It keeps working even
when the wifi is bad rink-side: anything you submit is saved on your phone
and sends itself when the signal comes back.

> Tip: the first time you open the site on your phone, a navy banner may
> appear at the bottom offering to install. Following that banner does the
> same thing as the steps below.

---

## iPhone (Safari)

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Safari open on the login page, Share button highlighted -->

1. Open **Safari** and go to the Rink Reports website (use the link your
   manager sent you).
2. Log in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **Share** button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the
   bottom center of the screen.
4. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
6. Close Safari. You'll see the **RinkReports** icon (white snowflake on
   navy) on your home screen.
7. From now on, open the app from that icon — not from Safari.

**Important:** this only works in Safari. If you use Chrome on your iPhone,
open the site in Safari for this one step.

---

## Android (Chrome)

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Chrome open on the login page, install banner visible -->

1. Open **Chrome** and go to the Rink Reports website (use the link your
   manager sent you).
2. Log in with your email and password.
3. A banner should appear offering to **Install app** — tap it and confirm.
4. If you don't see the banner: tap the **⋮** menu (three dots, top-right),
   then tap **Add to Home screen** (on some phones it says **Install app**),
   then confirm.
5. You'll see the **RinkReports** icon on your home screen.
6. From now on, open the app from that icon.

---

## After Installing

- **Stay logged in.** Log in once and the app remembers you. If it ever
  asks you to log in again, just sign back in — your saved reports are not
  lost.
- **Weak signal is okay.** Submit your reports normally. If you're offline,
  the app saves them on your phone and shows a pending count; they send
  automatically when you're back in signal.
- **Open the app at least once per shift.** This keeps everything synced
  and up to date.
- **Don't delete the icon** — removing the app from your home screen can
  delete reports that haven't synced yet. If you need to reinstall, make
  sure the pending count is zero first.

Questions? Ask your facility admin.
